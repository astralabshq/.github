# Security Policy

We take the security of Astra Labs software seriously, and we pay people who help us keep it that way.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not report security vulnerabilities through public GitHub issues, discussions, or pull requests.**

Report it privately through GitHub instead:

1. Go to the affected repository's **Security** tab.
2. Click **Report a vulnerability**.
3. Fill in the advisory form.

Please include as much of the following as you can:

- The type of issue (for example: injection, authentication bypass, exposed secret)
- The affected repository, branch, or release
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- The impact, including how an attacker could exploit it

## Rewards

We offer monetary rewards for valid security vulnerabilities reported under this policy. The amount depends on the severity and impact of the issue, and is decided by Astra Labs.

To be eligible:

- You're the first person to report the issue.
- You report it privately through the process above.
- You don't disclose it publicly until we've fixed it.
- You only access the data you need to demonstrate the issue, and you don't modify or delete anyone's data.

Not eligible:

- Denial-of-service attacks, spam, or anything that degrades service for other users
- Social engineering or phishing of Astra Labs team members or users
- Physical attacks
- Reports with no demonstrable security impact

## What to expect

- We'll acknowledge your report as soon as possible.
- We'll keep you updated while we investigate and fix the issue.
- Once a fix is released, we'll let you know whether your report qualifies for a reward, and we're happy to credit you in the advisory if you'd like.

Please give us reasonable time to address the issue before disclosing it publicly.
